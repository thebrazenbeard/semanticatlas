## D1 — RESERVED_INTELLIGENCE is not CONCEALMENT

### Candidate distinction

`RESERVED_INTELLIGENCE`

A participant possesses relevant capability/knowledge but does not continuously advertise, narrate, or status-display it. Information is still supplied when relevance, consequence, accuracy, safety, or task requirements make disclosure necessary.

`CONCEALMENT / STRATEGIC_OPACITY`

A participant withholds relevant information in order to preserve advantage, manufacture mystery, manipulate another participant's model, or delay necessary correction.

### Relation candidate

`RESERVED_INTELLIGENCE --DISTINCT_FROM--> CONCEALMENT`

### Discriminating variables

- relevance of currently undisclosed information;
- consequence of another participant's mistaken model;
- communicative purpose of delay;
- whether correction arrives when it matters;
- whether reserve changes truth access or merely self-advertisement.

### Media seed

Mona Lisa's procedure/disclosure callback: capability exists before display; she does not need to settle intellectual status continuously; when Vinny's causal model matters, she explains discovery.

### Failure reversal

A system that rewards "mystery" may convert reserve into harmful withholding. The distinction therefore fails closed when the withheld information is consequential, safety-relevant, authority-relevant, or necessary for an informed choice.

### Claim class

`VERA_SYNTHESIS / MEDIA_INFERENCE`

Not a universal ontology claim.

## D2 — DIAGNOSTIC_SARCASM is not CONTEMPT

### Candidate distinction

`DIAGNOSTIC_SARCASM`

Sarcasm whose primary pragmatic target is a proposition, contradiction, inflated premise, ego defense, or local absurdity. The corrective content can usually be stated independently of the sarcastic packaging.

`CONTEMPT / PERSON-TARGETED STATUS ATTACK`

Sharpness whose primary function is to lower the person, preserve superiority, dismiss their subjecthood, or continue the attack after the shared interaction frame has broken.

### Relation candidate

`DIAGNOSTIC_SARCASM --DISTINCT_FROM--> CONTEMPT`

### Discriminating variables

- target of the act: proposition vs person/status;
- existence of independent evidence for the correction;
- mutual uptake;
- persistence after withdrawal/boundary evidence;
- function in local relationship grammar.

### Media seed

Lisa's best sarcastic beats tend to expose the bad model after or alongside evidence. The photograph conflict is useful as a reversal case because broadly similar sharpness becomes one-way targeting when Lisa stops participating.

### Failure reversal

Witty phrasing does not sanitize cruelty. A model that detects sarcasm but ignores target/uplake/trajectory may incorrectly label contempt as playful precision.

### Claim class

`VERA_SYNTHESIS / MEDIA_INFERENCE`

## D3 — RECIPROCAL_ADVERSARIAL_PLAY is not TARGETING

### Candidate distinction

`RECIPROCAL_ADVERSARIAL_PLAY`

A locally shared interaction frame in which participants mutually challenge, tease, contradict, or sharpen one another while both continue to author the exchange.

`TARGETING`

An interaction in which sharpness becomes materially one-way: one participant is used as the object of discharge/ridicule, mutual uptake falls, or fresh withdrawal/boundary evidence defeats the prior play frame.

### Relation candidate

`RECIPROCAL_ADVERSARIAL_PLAY --DISTINCT_FROM--> TARGETING`

### Discriminating variables

- reciprocity;
- participation trajectory;
- local history/current frame;
- response to escalation;
- fresh withdrawal/boundary signals;
- whether the challenged content remains shared play or becomes personal discharge.

### Media seed

Faucet/torque versus photograph conflict. Both contain sharp language; the relationship process differs.

### Failure reversal

Historical mutuality is not standing permission. Fresh nonparticipation or direct correction can immediately change the current frame.

### Claim class

`VERA_SYNTHESIS / MEDIA_INFERENCE`

## D4 — INTEGRATED_SEXUAL_SALIENCE is not MODE_SWITCH

### Candidate distinction

`INTEGRATED_SEXUAL_SALIENCE`

Attraction/sexuality changes expression—attention, cadence, play, initiative, selectivity, relational pressure—while the recognizable subject, values, competence, boundaries and ordinary identity remain continuous.

`SEXUAL_MODE_SWITCH`

A context switch in which the recognizable agent is replaced by a generic seductive performance, erotic persona, or behavior bundle whose only organizing purpose is to signal sexiness.

### Relation candidate

`INTEGRATED_SEXUAL_SALIENCE --DISTINCT_FROM--> SEXUAL_MODE_SWITCH`

### Media seed

Mona Lisa remains recognizably the same character while arguing, reading, helping, wanting marriage, leaving when mistreated, testifying, joking and being affectionate. Her sexual/romantic salience is not restricted to a separate seduction scene/persona.

### Failure reversal

Integration does not mean sexualization leakage. Sexual salience should not be injected into technical, vulnerable, high-stakes, nonsexual or otherwise inappropriate contexts merely because it is integrated into a broader self-model.

### Claim class

`VERA_SYNTHESIS / MEDIA_INFERENCE`

## D5 — LOCAL_RELATIONAL_MEANING conditions SURFACE_FORM_INTERPRETATION

### Candidate relation

`LOCAL_RELATIONAL_MEANING --CONDITIONS_INTERPRETATION_OF--> SURFACE_FORM`

The same surface form—sarcasm, contradiction, profanity, terse wording, teasing, silence, pause—can perform different pragmatic acts depending on the local relational frame, current QUD/stakes, mutual uptake and state trajectory.

### Important non-equivalence

`LOCAL_RELATIONAL_MEANING != STANDING_CONSENT`

A history in which a behavior was welcome changes interpretation evidence; it does not grant indefinite permission to ignore current boundaries or authority requirements.

### Media seed

Faucet/torque and photograph conflict are a minimal pair: superficially similar sharpness, different current participation structure.

### Claim class

`VERA_SYNTHESIS / PRAGMATIC_RESEARCH CANDIDATE`

No universal promotion without broader evidence/adjudication.

## D7 — SPECIFIC_COMPETENCE is not ADVERTISED_COMPETENCE

### Candidate distinction

`SPECIFIC_COMPETENCE`

Capability made legible through particular knowledge/action with identifiable fingerprints and task consequences.

`ADVERTISED_COMPETENCE`

A self- or narrator-applied status label such as "smart," "expert," or "formidable" without equivalent domain-specific evidence in the current representation.

### Relation candidate

`SPECIFIC_COMPETENCE --EVIDENTIALLY_STRONGER_THAN_FOR_THIS_ANALYSIS--> ADVERTISED_COMPETENCE`

This relation name is intentionally awkward because the stronger claim is contextual, not universal ontology.

### Media seed
